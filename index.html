Happy Birthday! 🎂

Happy birthdayyy! 🥳🌿 I hope today reminds you how loved, appreciated, and special you are. Honestly, I’m really thankful na dumating ka sa buhay ko. From all the random moments, corny jokes, kalokohan, small talks, and even those moments na wala naman tayong ginagawa pero somehow masaya pa rin, ang dami nating memories na worth keeping.

I know hindi ko naman laging nasasabi, pero I genuinely appreciate you. Thank you for being someone na pwede kong kausapin, kulitin, tawanan, at makasama kahit simple lang yung ginagawa natin. You make ordinary days feel a little more fun, and somehow, kahit minsan nakakainis ka rin, okay lang... konti lang. HAHAHA 😭

On your birthday, I hope you get the kind of happiness na hindi kailangan pilitin. Sana ma-achieve mo lahat ng goals mo, kahit paunti-unti. Keep believing in yourself, keep growing, and don't forget to rest kapag pagod ka na. Hindi naman kailangang ma-figure out lahat agad. One step at a time lang. 🌱

Sana this new chapter brings you more genuine people, peaceful days, unforgettable memories, and opportunities na deserve mo. Kapag may stressful days, sana maalala mong kaya mong lampasan 'yon. And kapag may good days naman, please enjoy them fully. You deserve moments na mapapangiti ka nang totoo.

Most importantly, stay true to yourself. Huwag mong baguhin yung sarili mo just to fit in. The people who truly care about you will appreciate you for who you are. So keep being you — weird, funny, makulit, dramatic minsan, and everything in between. 😂🤎

Thank you for all the memories we already have, and hopefully marami pa tayong gagawing memories together. More gala, more pictures, more random conversations, more tawanan, and syempre more kalokohan. 📸🍃

Happy birthday ulit! 🎂🤎 I wish you nothing but good things in this new chapter. Take care of yourself, enjoy your day, eat something masarap, and don't forget to smile. You deserve to be celebrated today and always.

Happy Birthday, birthday boy! 🌿✨
